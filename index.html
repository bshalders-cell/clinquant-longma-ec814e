Meet Brent

Hi, I'm Brent, a wedding photographer based in Melbourne

I'm Brent, your devoted storyteller and visual architect, specialising in capturing the timeless beauty and cherished moments of your special day.

There's something truly magical about weddings – the love, the laughter, the tears of joy – and I feel incredibly privileged to be able to document these beautiful days. I strive to create photographs that not only reflect the essence of your love story, but also evoke the emotions and memories that will last a lifetime. From the tender exchange of vows to the jubilant celebrations on the dance floor, every wedding is a unique tapestry of emotions, and I'm dedicated to weaving each thread into a visual masterpiece that you'll treasure forever.

I understand that your wedding day is one of the most important chapters in your love story, and I will always approach it with the attention to detail it deserves. My goal is not just to capture beautiful images, but to create a seamless and stress-free experience for you and your loved ones, allowing you to fully immerse yourselves in the magic of the moment.

So whether you're planning an intimate elopement, a grand celebration, or anything in between, I'm here to bring your vision to life and create photographs that you will cherish forever.

— Brent

The experience

What it's like to work together

let's make sure it feels good from the start.

Before the day

  • Within a week of booking you'll receive a welcome guide with planning tips, timeline templates, and answers to the questions everyone asks.
  • I'm here on email or WhatsApp the whole way through — no question too small.
  • Where I can, I'll visit your venue beforehand to scout the best light and spots for portraits and group photos.
  • We'll plan your photography timeline together so the day runs itself.

On the day

  • I work quietly and stay out of the way — most of the day you'll forget I'm there.
  • Group photos are kept simple: 15–20 minutes for a handful of shots, so you get back to your guests sooner.
  • For couple portraits I'll gently direct you where it helps, so the photos look natural because they are.
  • If the run sheet slips, I adjust. Your timeline is my problem to manage, not yours.

After the day

  • Sneak peeks land within 48 hours, while the day is still fresh.
  • Your full, carefully edited online gallery arrives within six weeks — easy to download, print, and share.
  • I'll keep you posted at every step, so you're never left wondering where things are at.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do you approach a wedding day?

The goal is simple: capture the key moments with minimal fuss, and give you as much time with your guests as possible.

Group photos take 15–20 minutes for a handful of combinations, and couple portraits around 20–30 minutes at the best light of the day. Everything else is candid.

What's your style of photography?

Documentary at heart with an editorial finish. Through the day I focus on candid moments — the emotion and connection between you and your people, unposed. For portraits, I'll ask questions and give gentle direction so you feel comfortable and look like yourselves.

Do you do group shots?

Absolutely — they matter. I just recommend keeping the list short (five to eight combinations) so you spend more of the day celebrating and less of it lining up. If you do have a longer list, appoint a friend or family member to help coordinate, which keeps things moving smoothly.

When do we get our photos?

Sneak peeks arrive within 48 hours. Your full edited gallery — typically several hundred images for a full day — is ready within six weeks, delivered as an online gallery you can download, print from, and share with family and friends.

Do you photograph events as well as weddings?

Yes — corporate events, galas, awards nights, and private celebrations across Melbourne. Same approach: unobtrusive coverage, fast turnaround, and a gallery your team will actually want to use.

Do you offer videography as well?

I focus solely on photography, but I work alongside some amazing videographers and am happy to recommend trusted professionals who share my style and approach.

Do you travel?

All over Victoria — the Yarra Valley, Mornington Peninsula, Daylesford, Bellarine, and beyond. Travel within greater Melbourne is always included; for further afield I'll quote travel transparently up front.

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings, most couples book 9–18 months in advance, especially for popular dates. I recommend reaching out as soon as you've locked in your venue.

How do we book you?

Get in touch with your date and venue, and I'll send through my availability and full price guide. From there we'll chat through your options, you pay the deposit, and your date is locked in.

Can we request specific shots?

Absolutely! Before the wedding, I'll ask you to share must-have shots and family combinations. On the day, I'll balance capturing candid moments with making sure your key requests are covered.

Can we meet before the wedding?

Of course! I always encourage multiple meetings (in person or via video call) before your wedding so we can get to know each other, chat through your plans, and make sure you feel comfortable in front of the camera.

Do you work with a second photographer?

I am a solo photographer, but upon request can include a second photographer for an additional fee. I have a trusted network of professional photographers I can call on if this is requested.
